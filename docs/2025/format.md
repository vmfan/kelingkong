# Kelingkong 2025 — "Criss-Cross Hong Kong", as it ran

Reconstructed from `Old Kelingkong (2025)/Kelingkong 2025.xlsx`, tabs *Rules & Point
System*, *Score Sheet* and *Landmarks*. Where the tabs disagree, see `data-audit.md` —
this document describes the intended design, not the corrected one.

## Grouping

21 groups of 10. Each group had 2–3 leaders drawn from helpers, ranting, or PPI HK.
Separately, 9 staff ran the game posts.

## The lottery

Each group sent one person to draw a lot. The draw decided which game post the group
would visit *first*. Posts were only open at certain times, so the draw functioned as a
staggering mechanism to stop all 21 groups arriving at the same post simultaneously.

Every group had to visit at least one game post. Visiting none cost **−10 points**.

## The three game posts

| Post | Location |
|---|---|
| Post 1 | Tamar Park |
| Post 2 | M+ |
| Post 3 | Kai Tak |

Groups played a game at the post and scored for winning. The two tabs disagree on the
values — the Rules tab says 10 / 15 / 20 and the Score Sheet says 15 / 20 / 25. The Score
Sheet values are the ones that appear to have been tallied on the day.

Groups placing in the overall top 5 at a post earned additional points.

## The landmark maps

Each group received **three versions of a landmark map**, one per region. The three
regions were anchored on the game posts:

- **HK Island** (Pos 1: Tamar Park) — 17 landmarks, 1–4 points each
- **TST** (Pos 2: M+) — 17 landmarks, 1–5 points each
- **Kai Tak** (Pos 3: Kai Tak) — 14 landmarks, 3–8 points each

Point values scaled with distance from base: Hong Kong Island landmarks sat 5–18 minutes
from BNI Admiralty and scored 1–4; Kai Tak landmarks sat 33–45 minutes away and scored
3–8. **The scoring was distance-compensating** — this is the most important design idea
in the 2025 system and the reason groups had any incentive to leave Hong Kong Island.

Visiting **at least 2 landmarks in every region** earned a **+30 bonus**.

## Riddles

Some landmarks were named plainly on the map; others were given as crosswords or riddles.
Groups were not required to solve them, but riddle landmarks carried the highest point
values.

## Earning points

1. **Group photo** at a landmark. **Every member had to appear in the photo** for it to
   count.
2. **Winning games** at the three posts.
3. **Surprise missions** sent out by the committee during the day — five slots on the
   score sheet at 10 points each.
4. All photos had to be submitted **before 16:30**.

## Winning

Highest total points took "Criss-Cross Hong Kong". Scoring was tallied by hand on a
per-group sheet.

## Other material on the sheets

**Objects to Find** — a scavenger list separate from the landmarks: Cha Chaan Teng,
DimSum, Bolo Bun, Egg Waffle, Mango Mochi, Dai Pai Dong, Milk Tea.

**Finer district grouping** — the *Landmarks* tab organises the same places by MTR
district (TST, Mongkok, SSP, Kowloon, CWB, Central, Wan Chai, Admiralty, Sheung Wan,
Kennedy Town, Choi Hung, Lok Fu, Diamond Hill, Kai Tak, Whampoa) rather than by the three
scoring regions. This finer structure went unused in 2025 but became the basis of the
2026 board.
